## Replacing a photo with a real clinic photo

Real photos of the clinic and team always beat stock for trust and for Google Business Profile consistency. To swap one, drop the file in `public/images/`, then point the matching entry in `lib/treatments/media.ts` at it and update its `alt` text. Nothing else needs to change.
