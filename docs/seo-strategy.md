## Diagnosis

Eight problems hold the profile back, ranked by how much fixing each one moves the ranking.

1. **Reviews.** The client has 0–10; competitors average 511. This is the largest gap. Rank 15 has only about 16 reviews, which shows proximity and relevance can beat volume, but 0–10 can't compete.
2. **No website.** The domain in the site code does not resolve, so the GBP has no website link. Google gets no on-page signals about services or location.
3. **No directory listings.** The web check found no Practo, Justdial, Bajaj Finserv Health, DocIndia or Latrexa listing for the clinic. Ranking competitors appear on these sites.
4. **Primary category.** "Dentist" is set today. 15 of 20 competitors (75%) use "Dental clinic", which matches the main search "dental clinic in Hadapsar".
5. **Business name.** "…in Hadapsar | Root Canal | Aligners & Braces | Dental Implants" is keyword stuffing under Google's guidelines.
   - On a new profile with few reviews this risks suspension. A suspended profile shows nowhere and needs video re-verification.
   - Any competitor can report it through Google's redressal form.
   - The name must match the permanent signage.
6. **Split hours.** The clinic closes from 2 to 6 PM. Many competitors open 10 AM to 8 or 10 PM, so the client drops out of "open now" results every afternoon. Only extend hours the clinic will actually keep.
7. **The website disagrees with the GBP.** Hours, map coordinates, one phone number and "Viman Nagar" text all conflict with the profile. Consistent name, address, phone and hours everywhere is a trust signal.
8. **Dense competition.** At least 8 competitors sit within about 1.5 km in Amanora Park Town and Magarpatta. Expect to win the closest 1 km first, then widen the radius.

### Service-page template

Follow the existing root canal page design.

- **Title tag (60 characters max):** "Service in Hadapsar, Pune | Dr. Gayatri's Dental Clinic".
- **H1:** "Service in Hadapsar".
- **Meta description:** 150–160 characters with the keyword, a benefit and a call to action.
- **Length:** 900–1,400 words in these sections:
  1. What the treatment is.
  2. When you need it (symptoms).
  3. The procedure, step by step.
  4. Benefits.
  5. Cost range in ₹, from the clinic's real prices. Cost searches are among the highest-volume.
  6. Why patients from Hadapsar and Magarpatta choose the clinic.
  7. 5–8 FAQs taken from Google's "People also ask".
  8. A call to action and three links to related services.
- **Credentials:** a "Medically reviewed by Dr. Gayatri" line, with a date.
- **Structured data:** Service or MedicalProcedure, BreadcrumbList and FAQPage. FAQ rich results now show rarely, but the markup still helps Google understand the page.

### Compliance

The Dental Council of India's code of ethics discourages advertising claims of superiority and guarantees.

- Keep "best" as a research keyword, never as an on-page claim.
- Avoid "100% painless" and guaranteed results.
- Use before/after photos only with written consent.
